Notification cue sounds
=======================

Source: "Interface Sounds (1.0)" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
URL:    https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
         http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 places the work in the public domain, so no attribution is required. It is
recorded here anyway, so a future reader can tell where the three files came
from and re-check the terms rather than guessing.

Files, and which original each was taken from (converted to Ogg Vorbis, no
other processing — no trimming, no fades, no pitch/level changes beyond the
codec):

  cue_ping.ogg   <- bong_001.wav          0.13s  single soft blip
  cue_chime.ogg  <- confirmation_001.wav  0.29s  two-note confirm
  cue_alarm.ogg  <- error_003.wav         0.56s  longer, insistent

Keeping the originals named is the point: "we generated some tones" would make
them unreproducible and unattributable, and these are real recordings from a
pack whose licence is checkable.
